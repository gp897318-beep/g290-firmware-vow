A make.fun entry for [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929): one wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding, one sentence under 200 characters, judged pairwise by a stand-up-comic officiant until the funniest and most touching vow takes the $2.50.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` after validating it against the gate (1–200 characters, single line, not only whitespace):

> I vow to accept every firmware update life installs on us — even the ones that need a restart — because you are the one patch I will never roll back.
